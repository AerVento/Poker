Music has been excerpted into full-bar loops, blended at the loop boundary,
adjusted in level and encoded as Ogg Vorbis. Original MP3 files are retained
in AudioSources/Incompetech. Exact edits are in loop_edits.json there.
Licensing reference: https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/
The in-game Settings > Audio Credits dialog displays attribution and
identifies the loop edits. Keep these credits with redistributed assets.

SOUND EFFECT SOURCES

Casino Audio (1.1) by Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl)
https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Kenney samples are used in SFX_Bet, SFX_Call, SFX_Raise, SFX_AllIn,
SFX_Fold and SFX_Win. They were trimmed, mixed, faded, converted to mono
44.1 kHz PCM16 WAV and adjusted in level for this audition pack.
SFX_AllIn also includes a locally synthesized low tone.
SFX_Win also includes a locally synthesized ascending chime.
Exact source file mappings are recorded in AudioSources/sfx_manifest.json.
Original archive and license: AudioSources/Kenney/.

SFX_YourTurn, SFX_Check and SFX_Lose were synthesized locally for Poker,
using mathematical oscillators and deterministic noise; no external
recordings, paid generators or third-party voice samples were used.
The generation recipe is Tools/Audio/prepare_audio_review.py.

gift-egg-throw, gift-egg-splat, gift-champagne-pour and
gift-champagne-clink, gift-gun-rack and gift-gunshot are original local
synthesis, without external samples.
Recipe: Tools/Audio/build_gift_sfx.py; manifest: AudioSources/Gifts/manifest.json.

gift-bank-note-throw and gift-bank-note-impact are original local paper
foley synthesis, without external samples.
gift-bank-note-reveal is an edited excerpt (2.55-4.95 seconds) of a phone
recording supplied by the user. Original musical authorship is unspecified.
The original recording is preserved in AudioSources/Gifts/SwissBankNote/.
Edits: light noise reduction, EQ, compression, level adjustment and fades.
Recipe: Tools/Audio/build_bank_note_audio.py; parameters and source hash:
AudioSources/Gifts/SwissBankNote/manifest.json.

Slot-machine lever, reel-stop, cannon, small/medium/grand prize and lose cues,
and bgm-slot-spin are original local synthesis without external samples.
Recipe: Tools/Audio/build_slot_audio.py.
Manifest: AudioSources/SlotMachine/manifest.json.

bgm-slot-lounge is an original 132 BPM, 16-bar stereo party loop, synthesized
locally from brass, bass, bell, clap and disco percussion voices; no external
samples. Note and room tails wrap across the loop boundary.
Recipe: Tools/Audio/build_slot_music.py.
Parameters: AudioSources/SlotMachine/lounge-music.json.

gift-cigar-snip and gift-cigar-ignite are original local foley synthesis,
using deterministic noise and oscillators without external samples.
Recipe: AudioSources/Gifts/build_cigar_foley.py.
