SOUND EFFECT SOURCES

Casino Audio (1.1) by Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl)
https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Kenney samples are used in SFX_Bet, SFX_Call, SFX_Raise, SFX_AllIn,
SFX_Fold and SFX_Win. They were trimmed, mixed, faded, converted to mono
44.1 kHz PCM16 WAV and adjusted in level for this audition pack.
SFX_AllIn also includes a locally synthesized low tone.
SFX_Win also includes a locally synthesized ascending chime.
Exact source file mappings are recorded in AudioSources/sfx_manifest.json.
Original archive and license: AudioSources/Kenney/.

SFX_YourTurn, SFX_Check and SFX_Lose were synthesized locally for Poker,
using mathematical oscillators and deterministic noise; no external
recordings, paid generators or third-party voice samples were used.
The generation recipe is Tools/Audio/prepare_audio_review.py.
